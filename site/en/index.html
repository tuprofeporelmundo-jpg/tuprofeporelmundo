Frequently asked questions

What do I need for the online lessons?

A computer or tablet with a camera, a microphone and a good internet connection. We meet by video call.

I don’t know my level. Can I still book?

Yes. The booking form includes a free 12-question level test, and in the first 30-minute lesson I check your level and prepare your plan.

Do you prepare the DELE exam?

Yes. I prepare the DELE from A1 to C1 with a study plan towards your exam date, the four parts of the exam and exam-style tasks with correction.

Can I use my own textbook?

Yes. If you already have a textbook we follow it and I add my own material.

What if I can’t attend a lesson?

Tell me at least 24 hours before and we move it to another day. If you tell me later or don’t connect within 10 minutes, the lesson counts as done.

Is there a free trial lesson?

There is no free lesson. The first lesson lasts 30 minutes and costs 15 €; if you then buy a pack within 30 days, that amount is deducted.
