Learning Spanish online with a private teacher: what to expect

By Dana Salgado, native Spanish teacher from Spain

Apps are great for a few minutes of vocabulary a day, but most people only start really speaking Spanish when they practise with a person. A private online teacher gives you exactly that: a plan built around your goal and an hour a week of real conversation, with someone who corrects you.

How the first lesson works

In my case, the first lesson lasts 30 minutes. We get to know each other, I check your level and we talk about your goal: an exam such as the DELE, Spanish for work, moving to Spain or simply speaking with more confidence. With that, I prepare a plan and the material for your next lessons. If you are not sure about your level, there is also a free 12-question test when you sign up.

What a lesson looks like

  • You speak from the very first minute. Even at A1 level, we use the language in real situations.
  • Grammar when you need it. We look at the rules that help you say what you want to say – not a textbook chapter for its own sake.
  • Material for the lesson and notes to keep working at home. If you already use a textbook, we can follow it.
  • Daily practice between lessons. My students also get free access to “My classroom”, with 5 minutes of exercises a day that adapt to their level.

What you need

A computer or tablet with a camera and a microphone, and a stable internet connection. Headphones help a lot. That’s it – lessons are by video call, so you can learn from anywhere in the world.

How much it costs

Prices for private online Spanish lessons vary a lot, from very cheap marketplace offers to experienced specialist teachers. My prices are: first lesson (30 min) 15 €, single lesson (1 hour) 30 €, 5-lesson pack 125 € and 10-lesson pack 220 € (22 € per lesson). Lessons for two cost 20 € per person per hour, and small groups 17 €. See all prices.

How to choose the right teacher

  • A native speaker with teaching experience, not just someone who speaks the language.
  • A plan adapted to you. Ask how they will organise your lessons and how they will measure your progress.
  • Experience with your goal: exam preparation, conversation, or Spanish for work.
  • Clear conditions: prices, cancellations and how to pay.
